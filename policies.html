Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our services. The types of information we collect include:

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password, company name.
  • Payment Information: Billing details, payment method (processed securely by Stripe).
  • Content: Audio recordings, transcripts, and generated content you create.
  • Communications: Messages, feedback, and support requests.

1.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent, and interactions.
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device type.
  • Cookies: See our Cookie Policy below for details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide Services: Process recordings, generate content, and deliver outputs.
  • Improve Our Platform: Train AI models, analyze usage patterns, and enhance features.
  • Communicate: Send updates, notifications, and support responses.
  • Billing: Process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • Security: Protect against unauthorized access and fraudulent activity.
  • Compliance: Meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

💡 Note: We use aggregated, anonymized data to improve our AI models. Individual content is never used without your permission.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties who help us operate (hosting, payment processing, analytics).
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share.

4. Data Security

We take data security seriously and implement appropriate measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: TLS/SSL in transit, AES-256 at rest.
  • Access Controls: Strict team access with regular monitoring.
  • Security Audits: Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing.
  • Best Practices: Follow industry standards for data protection.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured format.
  • Object: Opt out of certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@fromple.ai.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion:

  • Content is available for download for 30 days.
  • After 30 days, data is permanently deleted.
  • Anonymized usage data may be retained for analytics.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

We operate in South Africa and may transfer data to countries with different data protection laws. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers.

Cookie Policy

9. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website recognize your device and remember information about your visit.

10. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Functionality: Enable core features like authentication and session management.
  • Performance & Analytics: Understand how visitors interact with our site.
  • Preferences: Remember your settings and language preferences.
  • Marketing & Advertising: Deliver relevant ads and measure campaign effectiveness.
  • Security: Help protect your account and prevent fraud.

11. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Essential for the website to function. Enable basic features like page navigation and secure login.

Performance & Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors use our site. We use this information to improve our content and user experience.

  • Google Analytics
  • Performance metrics

Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences and enhance your experience.

  • Language preferences
  • UI preferences

Marketing & Advertising Cookies

Help us deliver relevant ads and measure campaign success.

  • Retargeting
  • Social media tracking
  • Ad performance

12. Third-Party Cookies

We work with trusted third-party partners who may set cookies on our site:

  • Google Analytics: For website analytics.
  • Google Ads: For advertising and remarketing.
  • LinkedIn Insights: For tracking social engagement.
  • Stripe: For secure payment processing.

13. How to Manage Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to manage your cookie preferences. You can:

  • Block all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Clear existing cookies
  • Set cookie expiration

Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics Opt-Out
  • Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out

⚠️ Important: Blocking essential cookies may affect the functionality of our website. Some features may not work as expected.
