Getting Started

What is Fromple? ▼

Fromple is an AI-powered content strategy platform that transforms one recording (podcast, webinar, Zoom meeting, or interview) into a complete multi-channel content campaign. You record once, and we publish everywhere.

Our AI generates social posts, articles, newsletters, carousels, captions, hashtags, SEO metadata, CTAs, sales proposals, and more—saving you 20+ hours per week.

How does the free trial work? ▼

Our free trial gives you one complete campaign with no credit card required. You get:

  • 1 upload (max 45 minutes)
  • Full access to all features
  • 10+ generated content assets
  • 7-day publishing plan

Experience the full power of Fromple before you commit to a subscription.

What types of recordings work with Fromple? ▼

Fromple works with any audio recording:

  • Podcasts - Turn episodes into 10+ content pieces
  • Webinars - Repurpose into blog posts, social content, and lead magnets
  • Zoom/Teams Meetings - Generate meeting summaries, action items, and follow-ups
  • Interviews - Create case studies, articles, and social snippets
  • Presentations - Transform into slide decks, articles, and social content

Supported formats: MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC. Max 500MB per file.

What is the Brand Voice Engine? ▼

The Brand Voice Engine is our AI that learns your unique writing style from existing content. It analyzes:

  • Your website and blog
  • Newsletters and emails
  • Past campaigns and content

Every piece of content Fromple generates then matches your brand's tone, style, and voice—so your audience always recognizes you.

Available on: Growth plan and above.

Can I edit the generated content? ▼

Absolutely! You have full control over all generated content. You can:

  • Edit any piece of content directly in our editor
  • Regenerate content with different tones
  • Add or remove sections
  • Preview how it looks on each platform
  • Save drafts and publish when you're ready

We recommend reviewing all content before publishing—AI is powerful, but your expertise makes it perfect.

Technical

How does the AI processing work? ▼

Fromple uses a sophisticated AI pipeline:

  1. Your audio is transcribed using Groq Whisper Large v3 Turbo
  2. AI analyzes the content for key insights and themes
  3. Our Brand Voice Engine applies your unique style
  4. Content is generated across all platforms simultaneously
  5. Everything is organized into a 7-day publishing plan

Processing typically takes 5-10 minutes for a 30-minute recording. Free users may experience queue delays.
