The Discovery Engine

Four stages. One continuously growing registry.

Observe the market, classify the conditions, paper-trade both directions, and accumulate lessons from what actually happened afterward.

  1. 01 Observe

    Market State Capture

    Collect OHLCV, compute indicators locally, and read the current support, resistance, and structural state of the instrument.

    • OHLCV + symbol + timeframe
    • Configurable technical indicators
    • Support / resistance classification
    Explore Observe
  2. 02 Classify

    Condition Extraction

    Translate the raw market into a structured combination of technical, economic-calendar, COT, and fundamental conditions.

    • Technical condition set
    • Economic-calendar conditions
    • COT + fundamental conditions
    Explore Classify
  3. 03 Test

    Both-Direction Paper Trading

    Every occurrence runs as a long and a short experiment. Favorable and adverse movement are measured, not just win/loss.

    • Simultaneous long + short
    • Favorable / adverse movement
    • Movement distribution recorded
    Explore Test
  4. 04 Learn

    Registry Accumulation

    Statistics accumulate across observations. Individual conditions and combinations are analysed. Unsatisfactory setups are filtered.

    • Per-condition analysis
    • Combination analysis
    • Configurable setup filtering
    Explore Learn
