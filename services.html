Use cases

What Fromple does for you

Every industry shares the same problem — sending something sensitive to someone you don't fully trust yet, and losing visibility the moment it leaves your outbox. Here's how Fromple solves it, by role.

For founders raising rounds

Send your pitch deck as a tracked link. Know which investor read the financial model, which one skimmed page 3, and which one forwarded it to a partner before replying.

  • Per-page reading time on financials
  • Forward detection via new IPs
  • Revoke access mid-diligence if talks stall

For B2B sales teams

Send proposals and contracts without attachments. Your rep sees the moment the prospect opens the pricing page — and can follow up while attention is still warm.

  • Live activity feed on every link
  • Slack/email alerts when pricing is viewed
  • Identity gate captures name + company domain

For freelance agencies

Deliver client work through branded links instead of email attachments. Every deliverable is tracked, watermarked with the reviewer's identity, and revocable if the engagement ends.

  • White-label links on your own domain
  • Automatic watermark with reviewer email
  • Expiry dates for time-limited review windows

For M&A brokers

Run virtual data rooms without paying VDR prices. Assemble multi-file deal rooms, gate them by buyer domain, and log every document a party touches during diligence.

  • Multi-file deal rooms with folders
  • Domain whitelisting (e.g. only @acquirer.com)
  • Full audit log export for compliance
Capabilities

Six things email attachments can't do

Everything below works out of the box. No plugins, no browser extensions, no compliance review.

01

Protected canvas viewer

Your PDF renders inside a locked HTML5 canvas. No right-click, no copy, no download, no print command. The raw file never reaches the browser — only a signed render pass.

02

Per-page engagement

Millisecond-precision reading time on every page. See where attention peaks, where it dies, and exactly which slide lost the room. Not opens — actual engagement.

03

Instant revocation

Kill any link in one click. If someone is reading page 12 right now, their viewer locks and shows an access-revoked screen within seconds. No re-send, no awkward email.

04

Dynamic watermarking

Every page renders with the viewer's email, IP, and timestamp overlaid in a shifting translucent grid. If a screenshot leaks, the source is identifiable — and the shift defeats clean camera captures.
