About Fromple

We're on a mission to help creators and businesses scale their content effortlessly.

Our Mission

Fromple was founded with a simple belief: recording once should mean publishing everywhere.

We're building the future of content strategy—where AI handles the heavy lifting of content repurposing, so creators, consultants, and businesses can focus on what they do best: creating.

Our Story

Fromple started when our founders realized that creators were spending more time repurposing content than actually creating it. One podcast episode would take hours to turn into LinkedIn posts, Twitter threads, newsletters, and blog articles.

We asked: What if AI could do all of that in minutes?

That question became Fromple. Today, we help thousands of professionals save 20+ hours a week by transforming one recording into 10+ pieces of platform-optimized content.
