Observe the market, classify the conditions, test both directions, and learn from what happens.

The central loop

Observe. Classify. Test. Learn.
Then do it again.

  1. 01

    Observe & classify

    Market and fundamental data are processed into the current set of conditions.

    OHLCV · Indicators · Support / resistance · Economic calendar · COT · Fundamentals
  2. 02

    Identify or create a signal

    If the combination matches the registry, the existing setup is retrieved. If it doesn't, a new one is registered.

  3. 03

    Paper-trade both directions

    Long and short experiments run simultaneously. Favorable and adverse movement are measured and recorded.

  4. 04

    Update the registry

    Statistics accumulate. Individual conditions and combinations are analysed. Unsatisfactory setups are filtered.
