FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start sending.

Is there a free plan or just a trial?

The Sandbox tier is free forever — not a trial. You get 3 active documents and every core feature, no credit card required.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the current billing cycle. No contracts, no cancellation fees.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade any time. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades apply at the next cycle.

What happens to my files if I cancel?

You can export every document and analytics log before cancelling. We retain your data for 30 days after cancellation, then delete it permanently.

Do you offer custom enterprise plans?

Yes. For teams over 20 users, or if you need SSO, custom SLAs, or a dedicated region, contact us for a custom quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards. Enterprise customers can also pay by invoice with net-30 terms.
